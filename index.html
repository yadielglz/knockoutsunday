KNOCKOUT BLITZ OFFICIAL SPECIFICATION

All Schedules in America/New_York (Eastern Time)

1. Schedule & Weekly Competition:
• Mon–Sat: 11:00 AM–8:45 PM ET (Final Stretch & 3-Hour Power Hours: 5:45–8:45 PM ET).
• Sunday: 1:00 PM–5:45 PM ET (Final Stretch: 3:45–5:45 PM ET, 1-Hour Power Phase: 4:45–5:45 PM ET).
• Weekly Period: Sunday 7:00 PM ET to next Sunday 7:00 PM ET. Surviving stores at daily closing become Daily Qualifiers. Every store participates in every enabled daily event with cumulative weekly earned points carried over!
2. Point Scoring, Sales Milestone Bonus & 3-Min Cutoffs:
• 200 pts: Voice Line (VL), Watch (BTS), Tablet (BTS).
• 600 pts: HINT / HSI.
• Accessories: 2 points per $1 immediately ($25 = 50 pts, $50 = 100 pts, $100 = 200 pts).
• Sales Milestone Bonus: For every 300 points earned from sales, the store automatically receives an extra +300 bonus points!
• Battle Rounds: :10, :30, :50 each hour (5 mins each, max 2 attacks/store). Sales cutoffs are 3 mins prior (:07, :27, :47).
3. Combat, Deflection, Power Phase & Shields:
• Normal Attack: Cost 200 available pts → 400 damage.
• Deflection (Regular Hours after 120m): Survive ≥ 2 incoming attacks in minutes 0–120 and hold ≥ 1,350 available pts at round start. Reduces incoming simple attack to 100 dmg (cost 200). Same attacker can follow up in the same round with a Deflection Breaker (cost 400 → 500 dmg & breaks deflection for the round).
• Power Phase Entry: Requires ≥ 350 available pts at entry (5:45 PM Mon–Sat / 4:45 PM Sun). If all standing stores are below 350, the daily event ends immediately as a DRAW.
• Shields (Power Phase Only): Buy between rounds for 600 available pts (max 1 per upcoming round). Blocks all attacks for the first 3 minutes of that round.
• Self-Revivals (Before Power Phase Only): 200 available pts outside a round, 400 available pts during a round. Permanently disabled during Power Phase.
